Social Sphere was founded with one mission — to give Irish medium-sized businesses access to agency-quality digital marketing without the agency price tag or the agency runaround.

Our Story

We started Social Sphere because we were frustrated.

Too many Irish businesses were being let down by agencies that overpromised, underdelivered, and sent monthly PDF reports full of numbers that meant nothing. Clients were paying premium prices for average results and zero communication.

We built Social Sphere to do things differently. Every client gets a dedicated account manager, a private WhatsApp group with their full team, transparent reporting, and a free professionally built website included in every package.

No hiding behind email. No confusing reports. Just results, delivered by a team that genuinely cares.
